**HR Analytics Dashboard**

**#Project Overview**
This project analyzes employee data to understand attrition patterns, workforce demographics, and employee engagement factors. The goal is to identify key drivers of employee turnover and provide actionable HR insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

**#Business Insights**
Research & Development has the highest attrition count.
Sales Executives and Research Scientists show significant employee turnover.
Employees aged 26–35 form the largest workforce segment.
Life Sciences is the most common education background.
Employees who travel frequently show higher attrition compared to non-travel employees.
